## Section 3. The offering

[IMAGE: plain product photograph, brand name and any Kickstarter or campaign markings removed]

Please read this description, then look at the photo above.

This is a refillable bottle that sprays water under pressure. You fill it from any tap, bottle, or jug, then pump the top by hand about ten times to build up pressure inside. A hose pulls out of the top with a spray head on the end, so you can direct the water where you want it without holding the bottle upside down or squeezing it.

Specifications as given by the people who designed it:

- Holds 410 ml of water, roughly 14 fluid ounces, a little less than a standard water bottle
- Builds up to 3 bar of pressure by hand, roughly double the pressure of a typical household kitchen tap
- Sprays for about 120 seconds of continuous use per fill
- Weighs 205 g empty, roughly 7 ounces
- Retractable hose, 60 cm, roughly 2 feet
- Spray head adjusts between a narrow jet and a wide mist, and attaches magnetically to the bottle when not in use
- No batteries, no charging, no plumbing connection

What it does not do. It is not connected to a water supply, so it holds one fill at a time and you refill it by hand. It does not heat water. It is not a shower and will not rinse a whole person. At 410 ml it holds less than a large drink bottle, so for bigger jobs you would be refilling it repeatedly.

What comes in the box: the bottle, the spray head, the hose, and a strap.
