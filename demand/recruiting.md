# Recruiting

Live form: https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeZC-ESJ7xf28o-djKrnmblJX-lRfMKAtsK4RXY7b-sdIMSyg/viewform

Built in Google Forms, published Sunday 27 September 2026. Microsoft Forms would not get past its welcome screen for my university account, so I switched.

## Method

Ask in person, then text the link to the person's phone so they can finish it on the spot or later. Every channel goes through the same age gate, and anyone under 18 is routed straight to the end.

Nothing I say or send names the product, the brand, Kickstarter, or a price. The recruiting message describes a survey about outdoor gear and nothing more specific, so nobody walks into the valuation question with a number or a category already in mind.

## What I say in person

> Hi, sorry to bother you. I'm a business student doing a short research survey about outdoor gear. It takes about four minutes, it's anonymous, and I'm not selling anything. Would you be up for it? I can text you the link.

If they say yes, I text the link from my phone right then.

## What I will not do

I will not tell anyone what the product is before they reach it in the form, say what it costs, or suggest what a "good" answer looks like. If someone asks what it's for, I say it's a student project on whether a product is worth making, and I'll explain more once they've finished.
