## The method, in my own words

Counting: at each price, count the respondents whose stated maximum is at least that price. Under yes/no that is the whole method, with no assumptions. Fitting: run a straight line through that count at every whole dollar from $0 to $85, which assumes one linear relationship across everybody. The profit-maximising price comes from multiplying (price − cost per unit) by the count at each respondent's stated price and picking the highest.
