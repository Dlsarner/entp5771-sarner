## Part 2. Two patterns

### Pattern already in use: sell before you build

If buyers will pay in full months ahead of delivery, then the venture can finance its production run from customer money rather than from equity or debt.

This turns on value captured. The product is not different because it was crowdfunded; what changes is who holds the cash and the risk during the gap between order and shipment.

What would settle it: whether the same buyers pay the same price when the product is in stock and shipping tomorrow. If pre-payment is only tolerated at a discount, then the pattern is not financing the business, it is buying the money at 25 percent interest. The campaign cannot answer this, because every buyer so far has bought at a discount. I can partly reach this. My survey asks people to name what they would pay for a product available now, and comparing that against the 51 dollar pre-order price tells me whether the discount is the cost of the pattern or just a launch tactic. What I cannot reach is the counterfactual of the same person offered both options.

### Pattern it could adopt: accessory attachment around a durable

If the accessories can be protected from generic substitutes, then selling the sprayer at a thin margin and earning on refills, mounts, larger reservoirs and replacement hoses captures more total value than a single sale does.

This turns on value retained. The whole pattern depends on holding the accessory revenue against imitators, not on creating anything new for the buyer.

What would settle it: whether a PORTALO owner buying a replacement hose or a larger bottle has to buy it from PORTALO. I think the honest answer is no, and this is the pattern I chose precisely because I could be wrong about it in a way I would notice. The product uses a hose, a strap and a spray head, none of which appear to need a proprietary fitting, and the comparable campaign POWASH already sells its water bag as a 17 dollar generic looking add on. The evidence is cheap to collect and I can get it this week: search for a compatible hose and bottle at the stated dimensions and see whether generic parts fit. If they do, the condition fails, the pattern is unavailable, and PORTALO stays a one sale business with the repeat purchase problem I flagged in decision.md. That is a real finding rather than a story.
